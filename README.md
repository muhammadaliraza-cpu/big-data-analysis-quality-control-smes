## Abstract

Small and Medium-sized Manufacturing Enterprises (SMEs) often face challenges in maintaining product quality due to limited resources, inadequate defect management practices, and the lack of predictive quality control tools. This project explores a technology-oriented, data-driven framework to improve manufacturing quality and operational efficiency through the integration of machine learning, data mining, and causal analytics.

The proposed framework combines multiple analytical approaches, including classification models, Association Rule Mining (ARM), causal discovery, and counterfactual simulation.

A Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) model is employed to classify defect severity and predict rework requirements. Association Rule Mining identifies recurring relationships between defect triggers and defect types, while causal discovery investigates potential underlying causes of quality problems. Furthermore, counterfactual simulation evaluates potential process interventions and estimates their likely outcomes to support data-driven improvement decisions.

The overall framework aims to advance predictive quality control, reduce rework, improve manufacturing processes, and support operational sustainability and long-term competitiveness in small and medium-sized manufacturing enterprises.

## Research Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. Develop a data-driven framework for improving quality control in manufacturing SMEs.
2. Apply machine learning techniques to classify defect severity and predict rework requirements.

4. Investigate underlying causes of manufacturing quality problems through causal discovery.
5. Evaluate potential process improvement interventions using counterfactual simulation.

## Methodology and Analytical Framework

The project integrates four major analytical components:

### 1. Machine Learning – Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)

A Multilayer Perceptron neural network is used for classification tasks involving manufacturing quality.

**Applications:**
- Defect severity classification.
- Rework requirement prediction.
- Identification of quality-related patterns through predictive modelling.

### 2. Association Rule Mining (ARM)

Association Rule Mining is applied to discover recurring relationships between manufacturing defect triggers and defect categories.

**Purpose:**
- Identify frequently occurring defect patterns.
- Discover relationships between process conditions and quality issues.
- Support investigation of potential defect-related factors.

### 3. Causal Discovery

Causal discovery techniques are incorporated to investigate underlying relationships and potential causes of manufacturing quality problems.

**Purpose:**
- Explore causal relationships among manufacturing variables.
- Move beyond simple pattern recognition.
- Support root cause investigation and quality improvement.

### 4. Counterfactual Simulation

Counterfactual analysis is used to estimate how potential process interventions may influence manufacturing quality outcomes.

**Purpose:**
- Evaluate hypothetical process improvements.
- Estimate potential changes in quality outcomes.
- Support evidence-based operational decisions.

## Overall Research Workflow

The project follows an integrated analytical workflow:

1. **Data Gathering:** Collect and organize manufacturing quality-related data.
2. **Data Preprocessing:** Prepare the data for subsequent analytical procedures.
3. **Machine Learning:** Apply MLP-based classification for defect severity and rework prediction.
4. **Pattern Mining:** Use Association Rule Mining to discover relationships between defect triggers and defect types.
5. **Causal Analysis:** Investigate underlying causal relationships through causal discovery.
6. **Counterfactual Evaluation:** Estimate the potential effects of alternative process interventions.
7. **Quality Improvement:** Translate analytical insights into recommendations for predictive quality control and process optimization.

## Key Research Contributions

The project brings together predictive modelling, pattern discovery, and causal analysis within a unified manufacturing quality control framework.

Its key contributions include:

- **Predictive Quality Control:** Applying machine learning to anticipate manufacturing quality issues.
- **Defect Severity Classification:** Categorizing defects according to their severity.
- **Rework Prediction:** Predicting whether manufacturing defects require rework.
